Notes

Which event is the target?

Each clip was cut around one target closure. (1) If the eyes are already closed or closing in the first frame, that closure is the target. (2) Otherwise it is the closure that begins about one to two seconds into the clip; blinks in the extra footage before and after it are not the target. (3) If several closures could be the target, or you see no real closure, choose Cannot label and say why in the notes. (4) Set onset and reopening on the event you labelled.

Judge visible movement only. Do not use clip length or a duration cut-off to choose a class. The labels say nothing about the person's alertness.
